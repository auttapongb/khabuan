## The market does not “adopt an app.” It adopts a sentence other people already say

Thai socials almost never praise architecture. They repeat **one line they can send a friend**.

| What they post | What it really means |
|----------------|----------------------|
| “ไม่ต้องอายเวลาทวงเงิน” (Lemon8) | Face is the product. The bot is a third person so I stay a good friend. |
| “เกรงใจเพื่อน ไม่กล้าทวง” (Pantip) | The old thread is older than ขุนทอง. The bot finally answers it. |
| “ขี้เกียจโหลดแอป” / App Fatigue | Phone is full. LINE is already open 40 times a day. |
| “เพิ่งรู้ว่าไลน์ก็ทำอย่างนี้ได้” (Lemon8) | Surprise is the hook. The screenshot is the ad. |
| “จดสำเร็จ” (Lemon8 screen) | Confirmation stamp is what gets screenshotted and reshared. |
| “น้องในทีมแนะนำ” (aomMONEY) | Distribution is a colleague, not a store listing. |
| “ทุกคนใช้เป็น” — staff of all ages (Pantip เซนได) | LINE is the only shared skill. Excel is not. |
| “เงินเดือนชนเดือน” (Pantip) | Survival money, not fintech hobby. |
| “มีเก็บตลอด… มั่นคงกับอนาคต” (Pantip ป้านวล) | Outcome story you can copy-paste with a promo code. |
| “ขุนทองออกไป” (Thumbsup) | Temporary guest. When the job ends, the bot leaves. Trust. |
| “หลักฐานโกหกไม่ได้” (DroidSans) | Proof without a fight. |

Ads say cashless / COVID / AI. Socials say **เกรงใจ, ขี้เกียจ, อาย, ลืม, ชนเดือน**.

## Twelve adoption reasons, in the order socials actually use them

1. **เกรงใจ is a national feature, not a bug.** Pantip has years of “ไม่กล้าทวง” threads. ขุนทอง wins because it is the person who can nag. Headline on Lemon8 is literally ไม่ต้องอาย.

2. **App Fatigue is policy now.** LINE named it at LCT25. Downloads −22%. Asking a club to install Convoy.app is asking them to fail.

3. **The unit of virality is a screenshot, not a landing page.** Lemon8 posts are chat screens: “จดสำเร็จ”, bill split, ยอดสุทธิ. If it does not look good in a 9:16 crop, it does not spread.

4. **A 3-second creator clip beats a bank PR.** ป้านวล × ความปาล์ม: thousands → hundreds of thousands in a week. The reel is the onboarding.

5. **Users become the affiliate.** Pantip ป้านวล posts include Pro codes. Zendaibill’s founder’s partner teaches on-thread. aomMONEY heard it from “น้องในทีม.”

6. **LINE is the only skill every age has.** Uthai Thani pancake café built เซนไดบิล because staff could not share Excel. “ทุกคนใช้เป็น.” Luxury clubs have the same mix: owners, partners, staff.

7. **Salary-to-salary is the emotion.** “เงินเดือนชนเดือน” / “มีเก็บตลอด.” They adopt because the first month *shows money leaking*. MadamFinney: สติ, not a ledger. “ก่อนหน้านี้เราหลอกตัวเอง.”

8. **Proof without a fight.** DroidSans: who paid is on record. Social wallet / e-slip QR. For MCG the equivalent is counts, not names.

9. **The bot is a guest.** “ขุนทองออกไป” / Remove. Adoption requires an exit. Permanent group readers feel like spies.

10. **Any bank, not the sponsor’s bank.** Pantip: even though it is KBank, every bank’s slip works. Lock-in kills share.

11. **Recurring social bills train the habit.** Netflix หาร, ค่าเช่า, ทริป. Once the bot is in one group, the next group is a 10-second invite.

12. **Free to start, cheap to keep, no ad in the chat.** Lemon8 lists “ฟรีและไม่มีโฆษณากวนใจ” as a reason to try. Paid Pro comes after the screenshot habit.

## What we do **not** copy from socials

- Public pay-speed coins / เจ้ามือ ranking (Pantip mentions ขุนทอง points — fights no-speed).
- Promo-code spam in a luxury club.
- Auntie slapstick, cat themes, “ดุหลาน.”
- Reading the club group the way ป้านวล reads a 1:1 chat.
